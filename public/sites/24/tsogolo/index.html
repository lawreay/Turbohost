About Tsogolo Hub

Who we are

Tsogolo Hub is a Community Interest project built by young people, for young people. It brings together an agriculture assistant, a health assistant, an education platform and a business support platform behind one light, low-data front door.

Digital skills, career guidance and entrepreneurship resources sit alongside a general AI assistant and a real youth community — announcements, events, opportunities and open discussion.

We're proud to support young people through their journey, from learner to earner, one honest answer at a time.

The Ntcheu Youth Innovation Challenge Team

Tsogolo Hub started as an entry to the Ntcheu Youth Innovation Challenge — a competition that asks young people in the district to design real solutions to problems they see every day.

This team chose one of the widest problems it could find: that agriculture, health, education and business advice all live in different, disconnected places, and most of them assume a connection or a phone that many young people in Ntcheu don't have. Tsogolo Hub is the team's answer — starting small, staying useful, and growing one phase at a time.

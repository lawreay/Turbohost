About the hotel

Executive facilities. A friendly atmosphere. Service that means it.

Set on 21 garden acres in the government and administrative heart of Lilongwe, Sunbird Capital pairs the polish business travellers need with an ease of manner that's distinctly Malawian. Guests are as likely to remember the staff by name as the room they stayed in.

The hotel is part of the Sunbird Hotels & Resorts group, which operates nine properties across Malawi. Capital Hill, the seat of government, is just steps away — making this the natural base for conferences, delegations, and executive stays in the capital.

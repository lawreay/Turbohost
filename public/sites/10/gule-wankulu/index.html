About

Gule Wamkulu, meaning "The Great Dance," is a traditional ritual dance of the Chewa people of Malawi, Zambia, and Mozambique. It is performed by members of the Nyau secret society and is recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

History

The dance dates back centuries and is deeply connected to Chewa beliefs, spirituality, and community life. Dancers wear elaborate masks and costumes representing ancestral spirits, animals, or moral lessons. Gule Wamkulu is performed during funerals, initiations, and important community ceremonies.
